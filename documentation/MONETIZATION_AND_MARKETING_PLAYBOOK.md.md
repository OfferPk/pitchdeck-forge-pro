## 1\. Market Opportunity & Problem

- **The Pitch Deck Tax**: Founders spend 40+ hours wrestling with generic presentation software, or pay \$1,500–\$5,000 to pitch deck design agencies for slides that look pretty but lack rigorous financial metrics and defensibility answers.  
- **SaaS Subscription Fatigue**: Generic presentation tools (Pitch.com, Beautiful.ai) charge \$20 to \$40 per user per month on ongoing subscriptions.  
- **PitchDeck Forge Pro's Value Proposition**: A specialized, investor-grade pitch studio with built-in readiness scoring, executive 1-pager generation, and interactive slide presentations for a single one-time price.
